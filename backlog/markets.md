- [ ] The wrong Zoom — comedy of errors — It's March 2020. Everyone is on Zoom. You want to buy Zoom shares, you type ZOOM, you buy, and it goes up. Your friend has never been prouder. One problem: the video company's ticker is ZM. You bought a small Chinese phone-parts company with the same name. So did thousands of others. True part (LEAD): Zoom Technologies (ticker ZOOM) rose many times over in early 2020 until the US regulator (SEC) suspended trading on 26 March 2020; verify the size of the rise and the SEC order. One thing to remember: read the company name, not just the letters. (planned Wed 18 Nov, evening)
- [x] Minus thirty-seven dollars — disaster — You buy oil because it's cheap. Then it's free. Then they pay you to take it. You have a thousand barrels arriving and nowhere to put them. True part (LEAD): on 20 April 2020 the US oil price (WTI, May contract) closed at about minus $37.63 a barrel: storage was full in the lockdown, and holders of expiring contracts paid to get out of taking delivery; verify from the exchange (CME) or the US energy agency (EIA). Stonks is delighted at the price. One thing to remember: a contract for oil is a promise to collect actual oil. (planned Thu 19 Nov, evening) (2026-11-19-minus-thirty-seven)
- [x] The onion king — period — Chicago, 1955. You buy every onion in the city. Then you buy all the onions that don't exist yet. You own 30 million pounds of onions and they are starting to smell. True part (LEAD): Vincent Kosuga and Sam Siegel cornered the US onion futures market in 1955–56, then crashed the price so a bag of onions cost less than the bag; Congress banned onion futures in 1958, and they are still banned (Onion Futures Act). Verify each figure. Stonks stacks onions. (planned Fri 20 Nov, evening) (2026-11-20-the-onion-king)
- [x] The genius — biopic — You discover gravity. You invent calculus. You run the Royal Mint. Because you're a genius. Then you buy shares in a company at the very top. True part (LEAD): Isaac Newton and the South Sea Company, 1720: he sold early at a profit, bought back in near the peak and lost a fortune (often given as about £20,000); the line about calculating the motions of the heavens but not the madness of people is attributed to him, so say "he is said to have said". Verify with a historian's account (the figure is disputed). Stonks in a wig, clapping. (planned Sat 21 Nov, evening)
