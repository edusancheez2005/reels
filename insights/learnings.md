# What works on @sonartracker.io (from our own numbers, and Edu's taste)

Updated 2026-11-01: 2,502 followers (−24 since 28 Oct), 14 reels in the data, 9 old enough to judge (median 1,591 views).
Reports in `insights/weekly/`. The daily run reads this before writing a script. Each line: what to do, how sure we are, and the
evidence. Edu's explicit calls on quality override the numbers. The weekly review rewrites this file; keep it under 12 bullets.

## Do
- Open on one big, concrete number with a stake for the viewer, in the first sentence, and show it on screen. [Likely] Since
  27 Oct every crypto reel that did this got 1,207–1,856 views; the three reels without it got 151–172 views with 52–61% skipping
  in 3 s (bitcoin-origin's question hook got 158 on the same day mempool-bots got 1,775). Not [Certain]: whale-wakes also led
  with a number (4,582 BTC) and got 184, and everything before 27 Oct sits ~150–180, so part of the jump may be the account, not
  the hook. n = 11 number-led vs 3 without.
- Make every reel to the reference standard: episodes/2026-10-29-landfill-story and episodes/2026-11-01-184-billion-bug (real
  people, real documents as receipts, story-specific scenes, the specific lesson). [Certain: Edu, 29 Oct and 1 Nov]
- Vary the format by concept; never the same format twice in a row. [Certain: Edu, 31 Oct]
- Keep reels 45–60 s with real substance. [Likely] Views don't depend on length (30 s landfill-drive 1,798 vs 56 s versions
  1,591–1,697), but watch time does: the 54–56 s story reels held 22.8–26.3 s on average vs 15.0 s for the 30 s cut. Edu's call too.
- Put in one plain, practical warning or fact someone would forward to a friend who holds crypto, tied to a live event when
  there is one. [Likely] Shares are the gap: 8 of 9 judged reels got none. The two reels about something happening that day got
  10 of the 13 shares in the data: binance-hack 6 (4.2 per 1k reach), mempool-bots 4 (3.0 per 1k). No "tag a friend" bait.

## Avoid
- Off-crypto topics. [Likely] Both were the weakest of their week: battery-lithium 151 views / 61% skip; ai-agent-hack (AI
  hacking, not crypto) 1,207 / 47%, the lowest and most-skipped reel since 27 Oct (n=2; ai-agent-hack still young, 1.8 days).
- Cartoonish stand-ins and filler visuals (big-head Character3D figures, the same 3D set in many scenes, a generic lesson).
  [Certain: Edu, "realistic, not flat"; flagged again in the wallet-words, bitcoin-one-dollar and 184-billion-bug reviews] Use a
  real-document name card or a grounded figure; keep text ≥40 px from edges and out of Instagram's bottom 20% and right column.

## Not working yet
- Follows. [Certain] 13,149 views on the 8 reels posted since the 28 Oct review, and followers still fell 2,526 → 2,502. I do not know why: the data
  has no follows-per-reel field. Strangers watch (95%+ of reach) but don't follow. Don't change the CTA on a guess; watching.
- Breaking out: nothing has passed ~1,900 views (best 1,856 = 1.2× median). Every crypto reel since 28 Oct lands in the same 1.2–1.9k band, hook
  skip 27–47%. [Likely] the band is the account's current reach; I do not know what lifts a reel above it yet.
