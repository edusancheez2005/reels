# What works on @sonartracker.io (from our own numbers)

Updated 2026-10-28 (afternoon): 2,526 followers, 6 reels. Reports in `insights/weekly/`. The daily run reads this before
writing a script. Each line: what to do, how sure we are, and the evidence. Samples are tiny: [Guessing] means "lean this way".
The weekly review rewrites this file; keep it under 12 bullets.

## Do
- Open on a big, concrete number with a stake for the viewer, in the first sentence. [Likely] Two reels did it and both took
  off: mempool-bots ("This morning, forty-six thousand bitcoin payments were sitting in a public waiting room. And bots can read
  every one.") 1,758 views, 32% skipped in the first 3 s; lost-bitcoin ("About three point seven million bitcoin are probably
  lost forever.") 1,563 views in 3 hours, 33% skipped. The three reels without it: 140–174 views, 48–62% skipped.
- Make reels 30–35 s (Edu's call, 28 Oct). [Likely] People watch 18–25 s on average: mempool-bots 18 s of 49 s, lost-bitcoin
  25 s of 54 s, Bitcoin-origin 13 s of 74 s. Shorter means more people reach the payoff and replay it.
- Stay on crypto. [Guessing] The off-topic battery reel was the weakest: 62% skipped in 3 s, 8 s average watch.

## Avoid
- Opening on a question nobody can answer ("Will Bitcoin go up, or down?"). [Likely] 51% skipped in 3 s, 140 views. The
  reviewer flagged the same hook.

## Not working yet
- Shares: 5 of 6 reels got none, and shares are the strongest reach signal. Put in one practical warning or fact people would
  forward to a friend who holds crypto, said plainly. No "tag a friend" bait. [Guessing]

## Plan for strangers, not followers
- Over 95% of each day's reach is non-followers; only 7–15 followers a day see anything (Instagram account insights,
  24–28 Oct). [Certain] So the first 3 seconds decide everything, and posting time chosen around followers won't matter.
- The follower base is 78% India and about 16% aged 13–17 (Instagram audience insights). [Certain] Not who Sonar sells to,
  and not who's watching. Don't tailor topics to it unless follower reach grows.
