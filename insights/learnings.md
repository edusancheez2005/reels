# What works on @sonartracker.io (from our own numbers, and Edu's taste)

Updated 2026-10-29: 2,526 followers, 7 reels. Reports in `insights/weekly/`. The daily run reads this before writing a script.
Each line: what to do, how sure we are, and the evidence. Samples are tiny: [Guessing] means "lean this way". Edu's explicit
calls on quality override the numbers. The weekly review rewrites this file; keep it under 12 bullets.

## Do
- Make every reel to the landfill-story standard (episodes/2026-10-29-landfill-story): a 3D character for the real person,
  real documents as receipts, story-specific 3D scenes, the specific lesson. [Certain: Edu, 29 Oct: "so much better... even
  if it might take longer, do this type of content"]
- Open on a big, concrete number with a stake for the viewer, in the first sentence. [Likely] Three reels did it and all three
  reached ~1,700 views with about a third skipping in the first 3 s: mempool-bots 1,767 / 32%, lost-bitcoin 1,733 / 34%,
  landfill-drive 1,739 / 32%. The reels without it: 140–174 views, 48–62% skipped.
- Make reels 45–60 s with real substance (Edu's call, 29 Oct: the 33 s landfill reel was "too short, barely any information").
  [Likely] Length wasn't what drove views anyway: the 33 s landfill reel and the 54 s lost-bitcoin reel did the same.
- Tell the story properly: introduce every person (who, why they matter), explain how it happened and what was tried, and end
  on this story's specific mistakes and the fix, with the why (script guide, "Story reels: Edu's standard"). [Certain: Edu]
- Stay on crypto. [Guessing] The off-topic battery reel was the weakest: 62% skipped in 3 s, 8 s average watch.

## Avoid
- Opening on a question nobody can answer ("Will Bitcoin go up, or down?"). [Likely] 53% skipped in 3 s, 149 views.
- Filler visuals: a timeline in every reel, "Illustration" captions on obvious graphics, the same 3D set in 5 scenes, a generic
  lesson at the end. [Certain: Edu, 29 Oct]

## Not working yet
- Shares: 6 of 7 reels got none, and shares are the strongest reach signal. Put in one practical warning or fact people would
  forward to a friend who holds crypto, said plainly, with the why. No "tag a friend" bait. [Guessing]

## Plan for strangers, not followers
- Over 95% of each day's reach is non-followers; only 7–15 followers a day see anything (account insights, 24–28 Oct).
  [Certain] So the first 3 seconds decide reach, and posting time chosen around followers won't matter.
- The follower base is 78% India and about 16% aged 13–17 (audience insights). [Certain] Not who Sonar sells to, and not who's
  watching. Don't tailor topics to it unless follower reach grows.
