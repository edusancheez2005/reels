# What works on @sonartracker.io (from our own numbers)

Updated 2026-10-28 by the first stats review (`insights/weekly/2026-10-28.md`): 2,526 followers, 5 reels judged, median 171 views.
The daily run reads this before writing a script. Each line: what to do, how sure we are, and the evidence. The samples are
tiny, so [Guessing] means "lean this way", not a rule. The weekly review rewrites this file; keep it under 12 bullets.

## Do
- Open on a live, concrete number plus a hidden actor or threat, all in the first sentence ("This morning, forty-six thousand
  bitcoin payments were sitting in a public waiting room. And bots can read every one."). [Likely] mempool-bots: 1,755 views,
  10.3× the median; 32% skipped in the first 3 s vs 48–62% for every other reel; the only reel with real shares (4) and saves (5).
  One outlier, so keep testing the pattern rather than copying the topic.
- Keep reels around 45–50 s. [Guessing] The 74 s Bitcoin-origin reel was watched for 12.8 s on average (17% of it); the
  44–49 s crypto reels held 37–39%.
- Stay on crypto. [Guessing] The off-topic battery reel was the weakest: 62% skipped in 3 s, 8.3 s average watch.

## Avoid
- Opening on a question nobody can answer ("Will Bitcoin go up, or down?"). [Guessing] 51% skipped in 3 s, 0.8× the median.
  The reviewer flagged the same hook.

## Not working yet
- Shares: 4 of 5 reels got none, and shares are the strongest reach signal. Give people a fact worth forwarding (about money
  they or their friends hold), said plainly. No "tag a friend" bait. [Guessing]

## Watching (not enough data yet)
- Posting time: every judged reel went up between 12:53 and 16:30 London, so there's nothing to compare.
- Stories: daily stories start with the 29 Oct reel. Compare reel reach on story days with the days before.
