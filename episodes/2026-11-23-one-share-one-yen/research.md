## Facts used (every on-screen and spoken claim)

1. **The typo: one share at ¥610,000 became 610,000 shares at ¥1.** JT-2013: "On Dec. 8, 2005, Mizuho Securities mistakenly placed a
   sell order for 610,000 shares in J-Com Co. for ¥1 on the staffing firm's debut on the TSE Mothers market". AP-2005: "It had
   actually intended to sell 1 share at 610,000 yen (or, $5,041)." FINEXTRA: "The broker had actually intended to sell one share at
   Y610,000." (AFP's first-day copy says 600,000 in one sentence and 610,000 in another; every later source says 610,000.)
   → Script: "You were asked to sell one share. You're selling six hundred and ten thousand, for one yen each. Tokyo, 2005. That share
   was worth six hundred and ten thousand yen. You swapped the numbers." Tags/screen: "610,000 × ¥1",
   "8 Dec 2005", "Tokyo".
2. **A warning appeared and the employee carried on.** JTW-2005: "Although a warning that the order was abnormal appeared on the
   computer screen, the employee continued the operation."
   → Script: "A warning pops up." The pop-up talking ("You sure?"), "I'm a professional" and "you have never read a pop-up" are the
   fiction about "you", not claims about the real employee.
3. **More than forty times the shares that existed.** JTW-2005: "about 42 times the 14,500 outstanding J-Com shares." AP-2005:
   "41 times that of J-Com's true outstanding amount". FINEXTRA: "more than 40 times the actual number". 610,000 / 14,500 = 42.07.
   → Script: "more than forty times the shares the company has" (the figure all three support; the safer number). Tag: "40× too many".
4. **Cancel attempts failed, several times.** AP-2005: "Mizuho says it tried to cancel the order three times". FINEXTRA: "tried to
   withdraw the order four times". JTW-2005: "tried several times to cancel the sell order. But the TSE's computer system would not
   accept the cancellation request." → Script: two attempts shown ("several" in every source; at least three); no count on screen.
5. **The exchange's own system had a bug.** JTW-2005: "the TSE admitted that a defect in its trading system had prevented Mizuho from
   canceling the order." FINEXTRA: "its dealing system is unable to cancel sell orders while taking buy orders"; "it now admits that
   its own trading system was partially at fault." RISK: "a fault in the exchange's trading systems made the problem worse by
   blocking Mizuho's attempts to cancel the trade."
   → Script: "The exchange's own system has a bug: it can't cancel your order."
6. **Others kept buying; Mizuho had to buy the shares back.** JTW-2005: "Mizuho bought back about 510,000 J-Com shares, although
   96,236 shares theoretically remained in the hands of investors"; those were settled at "¥912,000 in cash for each share."
   JT-2006: "Mizuho lost about 40.7 billion yen buying back all the shares". AFP-2005: the price rallied "apparently as Mizuho tried
   to reverse its mistake."
   → Script: "Strangers keep buying. Your firm now owes shares it never had, and must buy them at the real price."
7. **The loss: about ¥40 billion.** JT-2006: "about 40.7 billion yen". JT-2013: "more than ¥40 billion in losses." JTW-2005: "around
   ¥40 billion." RISK: "¥40 billion ($343 million)". (First-day estimate: "at least 27 billion yen", AP/AFP.)
   → Script: "About forty billion yen, gone." Tag: "≈ ¥40 billion". Caption: about $340 million at the time (RISK).
8. **The exchange's president resigned.** RISK (20 Dec 2005): "Tokyo Stock Exchange president Takuo Tsurushima and two other
   executives have resigned in response to the Mizuho trading error 12 days ago."
   → Not spoken (cut for length after the script review); caption only.
9. **The court made the exchange pay about ¥10.7 billion.** JT-2009: "The Tokyo District Court on Friday ordered the Tokyo Stock
   Exchange to pay about ¥10.7 billion in damages to Mizuho Securities Co., saying the bourse is more to blame"; Mizuho had sought
   ¥41.5 billion. JT-2013: "The Tokyo High Court on Wednesday upheld a 2009 lower court ruling and ordered the Tokyo Stock
   Exchange to pay about ¥10.7 billion" (the Quote card uses these exact words). MIZUHO-2015: "the Supreme Court
   rejected both our final appeal and our petition for final appeal"; "the judgment rendered by the Tokyo High Court ... has become
   final"; "10.7 billion yen compensation". 10.7 / 40.7 = 26%.
   → Script: "a court made the exchange repay about a quarter." Receipt on screen: JT-2013 (or a Quote card with its exact words).
10. Not used on screen: about 50 firms later handed back around ¥20 billion of profits to a dealers' fund (Finextra 14929); time of
    day of the order (no source read in this run gives it).
