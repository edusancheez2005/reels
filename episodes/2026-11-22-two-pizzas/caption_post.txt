In May 2010, a man paid 10,000 bitcoin for two pizzas. At bitcoin's October 2025 record, those coins hit $1.26 billion.
Laszlo Hanyecz offered 10,000 BTC on the Bitcoin Forum for "a couple of pizzas" with some left over for the next day. On 22 May 2010 a 19-year-old bought him two from Papa John's for about $25; the coins were worth about $41. He says he has no regrets: to him it was free pizza. It was the first time bitcoin bought something real, which is why 22 May is Bitcoin Pizza Day.
The suit guy and his friend are made up. The numbers are real.
