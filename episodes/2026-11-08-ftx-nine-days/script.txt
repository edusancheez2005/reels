A thirty-two billion dollar company, bankrupt in nine days: FTX, a crypto exchange where people stored coins.
Its founder, Sam Bankman-Fried, also owned trading firm Alameda.
News site CoinDesk revealed Alameda held billions in FTT, a coin FTX made itself.
If FTT crashed, so would Alameda.
Changpeng Zhao, boss of rival exchange Binance, said he'd sell Binance's FTT.
Sam tweeted: FTX is fine. Assets are fine.
Customers panicked, pulling out about six billion dollars in seventy-two hours. FTX froze withdrawals.
Binance offered to buy FTX, then backed out over mishandled customer funds.
Sam had sent customer deposits to Alameda.
On November eleventh, 2022, FTX filed for bankruptcy. Sam later got twenty-five years in prison.
Two things broke FTX. One man controlled both companies' money. Customers couldn't check theirs until it was gone.
