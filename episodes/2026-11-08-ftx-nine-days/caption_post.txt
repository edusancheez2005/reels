FTX was a $32 billion crypto exchange. Nine days after one news story, it was bankrupt. Here's how it happened.
On 2 Nov 2022, CoinDesk reported that Alameda Research, Sam Bankman-Fried's trading firm, held billions in FTT, a coin FTX made itself. Binance's boss, Changpeng Zhao, said he'd sell Binance's FTT. Bankman-Fried tweeted "FTX is fine. Assets are fine." Customers pulled out about $6 billion in 72 hours, withdrawals froze, Binance dropped its offer to buy FTX over "mishandled customer funds", and FTX filed for bankruptcy on 11 Nov. FTX had sent customer deposits to Alameda. He was sentenced to 25 years in 2024.
The lesson: one man controlled the exchange and the firm using its customers' money, and customers couldn't check their coins until it was too late. Keep only what you trade on an exchange, and the rest in a wallet only you control.
