## Script round 2 notes
- "If FTT crashed, so would Alameda": CoinDesk 6 Nov 2022 — $5.8B of Alameda's $14.6B assets were FTT incl. FTT pledged as
  collateral, so a falling FTT price shrinks Alameda's assets and its collateral (that's why the leak and Binance's sale mattered).
- "FTX stopped paying out": SiliconANGLE 8 Nov 2022, FTX "reportedly stopped processing withdrawals" (on-chain data, fact 7).
- "Sam had sent customer deposits to Alameda": DOJ fact 10 ("channeled billions of dollars in customer deposits from FTX to Alameda").
- "the firm spending customers' money": DOJ — used to "repay billions of dollars in loans owed by Alameda Research" and "for his
  personal use". Caroline Ellison's testimony (fact 11) cut from the voice for length/clarity.

## The lesson (this story's mistakes)
- One boss controlled the exchange holding customer money AND his own trading firm, so deposits could be moved to Alameda
  (facts 3, 10, 11).
- Customers had no way to check their coins were there; the shortfall only showed when everyone withdrew at once (facts 6, 7: SBF
  said "assets are fine" while ~$6B left and withdrawals stalled).
- Fix: keep on an exchange only what you trade; hold the rest in a wallet where you hold the keys (self-custody) — then no one else
  can lend out or move your coins. Not financial advice; no price talk.
