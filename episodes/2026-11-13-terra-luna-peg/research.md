## Facts (each used on screen or in the voice)
1. **$40 billion.** DOJ headline: "...Orchestrating $40 Billion Fraud"; body: "UST and LUNA crashed, resulting in over $40 billion worth in investor losses." SEC: "This wiped out $40 billion in market value nearly overnight." [DOJ][SEC] → hook "Forty billion dollars, wiped out".
2. **In a week.** UST began to lose its peg over the weekend of 7–8 May 2022 [BLOCK][CG]; $0.61 on 9 May, ~$0.30 on 10 May [BLOCK]; "plunging straight down to $0.20" in the 13 May update [CG]; LUNA below $0.001 by 13 May [CG]. SEC: "nearly overnight". → "in a week" is conservative.
3. **Built to always be worth $1.** DOJ: the Terra Protocol "purportedly used a computer algorithm to maintain the value of Terraform's so-called 'stablecoin' pegged to the U.S. dollar, TerraUSD ('UST'), at a value of $1 for one UST." [DOJ]
4. **Do Kwon.** DOJ: "DO HYEONG KWON", "KWON, 34, of the Republic of Korea"; "Terraform was a blockchain and cryptocurrency company co-founded by KWON in 2018." Subhead: "Do Kwon, Founder of Terraform Labs". [DOJ]
5. **The swap rule.** DOJ: "Terraform promotional materials claimed that, under the Terra Protocol, one UST could always be exchanged for $1 worth of LUNA... Conversely, $1 worth of LUNA could always be exchanged for one UST." [DOJ] The Block quoting Kwon: "at any given time a person can burn a dollar's worth of Luna in order to mint one TerraUSD". [BLOCK]
6. **Arbitrage kept it near $1.** CoinGecko: "if UST is trading at $0.98, arbitrageurs can buy it and redeem it for $1 of LUNA." [CG]
7. **USDC contrast (reserve-backed).** Circle: "USDC is always redeemable 1:1 for US dollars". Reserves $69.1B vs $68.9B in circulation, as of 9 Nov 2026 (cash deposits, overnight Treasury repo, <3-month Treasuries). [CIRCLE] (Shown as an example of a reserve-backed design, not a recommendation.)
8. **Anchor up to 19.5%; $14B.** CoinDesk: "Deposits on Anchor, which offered up to 19.5% yield on deposits, plunged to $8.7 billion on Monday from $14 billion on Friday" (Fri 6 → Mon 9 May 2022); "some $5 billion of deposits, or more than a third of the total, vanish over the past few days in what is the equivalent of a crypto-style bank run". [CD] The Block: Anchor "is supposed to pay out 19.5% yield". [BLOCK]
9. **New Luna flooded out.** CoinGecko 13 May: LUNA circulating supply "ballooned to 923B". [CG] (Mechanism: swapping UST in mints new LUNA, per fact 5.)
10. **$84 → under a tenth of a cent.** The Block (11 May): "Luna... is down to around $1.25, from $84 a week ago." CoinGecko (13 May): LUNA "has now fallen by another decimal place to below $0.001." [BLOCK][CG]
11. **May 2021 slip and the secret buyer.** DOJ: "KWON claimed that the Terra Protocol on its own had caused the successful restoration of UST's $1 value after it dropped below 92 cents in or about May 2021. That was a lie. In truth... KWON reached an agreement with executives at a high-frequency trading firm (the 'Trading Firm') to have the Trading Firm purchase large amounts of UST to artificially support UST's $1 peg." [DOJ] (The Block says $0.96 low in May 2021; we use DOJ's wording "slipped", no number on screen except DOJ's 92 cents in the screenshot.)
12. **Bigger next time.** DOJ: by May 2022 "the UST market was approximately nine times larger in terms of market capitalization" than in May 2021; "he was not able to do so in May 2022". [DOJ]
13. **Pleaded guilty; 15 years.** DOJ: "pled guilty in August 2025"; "sentenced to 15 years in prison" (11 Dec 2025) "for committing wire fraud and conspiring to commit securities fraud, commodities fraud, and wire fraud". [DOJ]
14. (context, maybe on screen) Peak value: "the total market value of all UST and ... LUNA, exceeded $50 billion" in spring 2022. [DOJ] SEC jury found Terraform and Kwon liable on 5 Apr 2024; they agreed to pay "more than $4.5 billion". [SEC] Arrested in Montenegro on or about 23 Mar 2023 "for trying to use a fraudulent passport". [DOJ]
