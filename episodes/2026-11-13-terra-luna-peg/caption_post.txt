$40 billion, gone in about a week, when a coin built to always be worth $1 broke. In May 2022, UST lost its peg and Luna went from $84 to under a tenth of a cent.
UST had no cash behind it. One UST could always be swapped for $1 of newly printed Luna, so traders kept it near $1. Then Anchor, an app paying up to 19.5% on UST, lost about $5B of deposits in days. Holders swapped UST for Luna, new Luna flooded out, and both crashed together. US prosecutors say that when UST slipped in 2021, Do Kwon told the public the system fixed itself while a trading firm was secretly buying UST. He pleaded guilty and got 15 years.
The lesson: before you hold a stablecoin, check what backs it (cash you can redeem, or another coin) and ask who pays the yield.
