The first bitcoin transfer ever: on 12 January 2009, Satoshi Nakamoto sent Hal Finney ten coins, just as a test.
Sending to a new address? Send a small test amount first. Even bitcoin's inventor did.
Hal Finney, a cryptographer who worked on PGP, downloaded bitcoin as soon as it came out, reported bugs while Satoshi fixed them, and switched off his mining because his computer ran hot and the fan noise bothered him. He later wrote that he wished he had kept it up longer, and that he was extraordinarily lucky to be there at the beginning. Nobody knows who Satoshi is.
The suit guy and his friend are made up. The story is real.
