## How it works (the beginner sentence)
Every bitcoin move is public on the blockchain, the reason never is. A move to a new address can be a security upgrade or new
storage; coins going to an exchange or a broker can mean a sale. Here the first move looked like an upgrade; three weeks later
the coins were sold through a broker. The one thing to remember: a move is not a sale; watch where the coins land.
