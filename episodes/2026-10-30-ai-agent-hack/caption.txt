In 11 days, one hacking group hit 200+ organisations in 14 countries, and AI did most of the work.
Security firm CrowdStrike says a China-linked group it calls Vapor Panda ran a team of AI agents, mostly on the Chinese model DeepSeek. One human gave the orders; the agents tried several ways in at once and switched when one failed. Targets included foreign ministries, defence and chip firms, and phone networks, with confirmed break-ins at nine. The eye-opener is speed: a human runs maybe 40 commands an hour, these agents could top 1,500. A year ago, the first known AI-run spy campaign hit about 30 targets. Now it's 200+. The boring basics still matter most: updates and two-step logins.
