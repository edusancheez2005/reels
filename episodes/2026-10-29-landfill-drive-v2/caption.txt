In 2013, James Howells binned a hard drive with the key to 8,000 bitcoin (his figure). Today: over $600 million.
He mined them on a laptop in 2009, kept the drive in a drawer, then threw out the wrong one. His backups didn't have the key. The council refused him a dig, and a judge threw out his £495M claim.
Two lessons: back up your recovery words in two places, and label any drive that holds a key.
