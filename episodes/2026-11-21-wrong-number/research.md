## Facts (each with its source)

1. **"Wrong number" texts are one of the top five US text scams of 2024.** FTC Data Spotlight, "Top text scams of 2024"
   (14 Apr 2025), top five: fake package delivery problems; phony job opportunities; fake fraud alerts; bogus notices about
   unpaid tolls; **"'Wrong number' texts that aren't"**. The five are "estimated to account for about half of all text frauds
   reported". https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/04/top-text-scams-2024
2. **How the wrong-number scam runs** (same FTC page): the text "might just say "hello" or "do you want to get a coffee?"";
   "these scammers strike up a fake friendship, often with romantic undertones."; "Next, they claim to be successful
   investors, offering to share their tricks and directing people to bogus investment platforms."; "People report losing all
   the money they "invest," often tens of thousands of dollars." (Script: the text, the romance, "she's rich from crypto,
   she'll teach you", "her app".)
3. **Fake profits, then fees and taxes to withdraw**: FBI IC3 2025 Annual Report, pp. 11–12: victims "are shown fake profits";
   when they try to withdraw "they will be charged taxes and fees as a final attempt to exploit money from the victims before the
   scammers disappear". Contact starts "through text messages, social media sites, advertisements, or dating applications".
   https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf (read 11 Nov 2026 for the pig-butchering episode; the report's
   crypto-investment total of $7.2B re-confirmed today by Fox Business:
   https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/crypto-fraud-tops-fbis-annual-crime-report-americans-lose-billions-scams). (Script:
   "the app says you've doubled it"; "first a fee, then a tax on the fee".) The "fee for the tax" third item is the joke's
   escalation of IC3's "taxes and fees", not a claim about one case.
4. **"Pig butchering" defined** in a court filing in the Hanes case: "a scammer convincing a victim (a pig) to invest in
   supposedly legitimate virtual currency investment opportunities" and then stealing the money. CNBC (Dan Mangan, 21 Aug
   2024) via NBC Washington:
   https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/business/money-report/cryptocurrency-pig-butchering-scam-wrecks-kansas-bank-sends-ex-ceo-to-prison-for-24-years/3699566/
   Script: "pig butchering: fatten you up with fake profits, then take everything" (fake profits = fact 3).
5. **The real case — who**: Shan Hanes, CEO of Heartland Tri-State Bank (HTSB) in Elkhart, Kansas. DOJ, District of Kansas,
   "Former CEO of failed bank sentenced to prison", Monday 19 Aug 2024:
   https://www.justice.gov/usao-ks/pr/former-ceo-failed-bank-sentenced-prison . He "began stealing after being targeted in a
   pig-butchering scheme in late 2022", via "an unidentified co-conspirator on the electronic messaging app 'WhatsApp'" (CNBC/NBC).
   We do NOT say he got a wrong-number text: the script says "the same scam" (pig butchering), and "a real bank boss in Kansas".
6. **Paying to unlock profits**: "the scammers insisted they needed more funds to unlock the supposed returns on his
   investments"; prosecutors: further investments "always require another need for funds, to guarantee or unfreeze the
   earlier transfers" (CNBC/NBC).
7. **$47.1 million of the bank's money**: "initiated 11 outgoing wire transfers between May 2023 and July 2023 totaling $47.1
   million" of Heartland's funds to a cryptocurrency wallet (DOJ). "over just eight weeks" (CNBC/NBC). Script: "forty-seven
   million dollars of his bank's money", "in 2023".
8. **Never a profit**: "Hanes never realized any profit, and lost all of the money he stole" (CNBC/NBC).
9. **The bank failed**: "Hanes' fraudulent actions caused HTSB to fail and the bank investors to lose $9 million." "The FDIC
   absorbed the $47.1 million loss." (DOJ). Closed 28 Jul 2023 (FDIC failed bank list:
   https://www.fdic.gov/resources/resolutions/bank-failures/failed-bank-list/heartlandtristate.html, from the 11 Nov research).
10. **24 years**: "sentenced to 293 months in prison" (= 24 years 5 months) after he "pleaded guilty to one count of embezzlement
    by a bank officer" (DOJ). NBC headline: "sends ex-CEO to prison for 24 years". Script: "He got twenty-four years."
11. He also used "$60,000 taken from a daughter's college fund" and "$40,000 from Elkhart Church of Christ" (CNBC/NBC). Not used
    (nothing cruel about a real person; the joke is on "you", not him).

## The steps (protection only)
- A stranger who brings up crypto: block them (FTC fact 2, IC3 fact 3: contact starts by text, then talk of investing).
- A fee or "tax" to withdraw means the money is already gone (IC3 fact 3).
