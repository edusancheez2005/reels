"Wrong number" texts are one of the FTC's top 5 text scams. Here's how one ends with your savings stuck in a fake crypto app.
The FTC says these texts start with a hello, build a friendship with romantic undertones, then steer you to a "bogus investment platform". The FBI says victims see fake profits, then get charged taxes and fees to withdraw. The same scam, pig butchering, took a Kansas bank CEO in 2023: he wired $47.1 million of his bank's money to scammers, the bank failed, and he was sentenced to 293 months in prison.
