# Hold the line — GameStop, January 2021 (evening skit, markets, 24 Nov 2026)

Topic source: plan row "Tue 24 Nov · evening". Checked 24 Nov 2026, 17:15–17:45 London. Every URL below came from a web search.

## Facts (each with its source)
1. GameStop (GME) closed at $19.95 on 12 Jan 2021 and $31.40 on 13 Jan. SEC staff report, p. 19: "rose to $31.40 from $19.95 the prior day". https://www.sec.gov/files/staff-report-equity-options-market-struction-conditions-early-2021.pdf
2. Highest close: $347.51 on 27 Jan 2021 ("closed at a high of $347.51 per share ... a more than 1,600% increase from its closing price on January 11"), SEC report p. 19. Intraday high $483.00 on 28 Jan ("The following day, share prices jumped further to an intraday high of $483.00"), p. 19.
3. Scores on the way: "another 27% jump the next day to $39.90" (14 Jan), Reuters timeline, 18 Feb 2021: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/timeline-the-gamestop-battle--how-it-unfolded-for-the-key-players-testifying-2422131 ; "The stock ended the day 92% higher at $147.98" (Tue 26 Jan), CNBC, 27 Jan 2021: https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2021/01/27/hedge-fund-targeted-by-reddit-board-melvin-capital-closed-out-of-gamestop-short-position-tuesday.html
4. Short selling: short interest reached 122.97% of the float in January 2021 (SEC report p. 22); "short sellers covering their positions likely contributed to increases in GME's price" (p. 26), though "it was the positive sentiment, not the buying-to-cover, that sustained the weeks-long price appreciation" (p. 27). So the script says buying back "pushes the price higher still", not that it was the main cause.
5. The buyers: individual investors, the subreddit WallStreetBets (SEC report p. 18); accounts trading GME went "from less than 10,000 at the beginning of the month to nearly 900,000" by 27 Jan (p. 21).
6. Melvin Capital: Reuters timeline, "Jan 25": "Hedge fund Melvin Capital Management receives $2.75 billion investment from Citadel" and Point72, after losses on "a series of short bets, including on GameStop". CNBC (27 Jan 2021, above): "Citadel and Point72 have infused close to $3 billion"; "Melvin Capital closed out its short position in GameStop on Tuesday afternoon after taking a huge loss".
7. Melvin "lost 53% in January" (reported by The Wall Street Journal, relayed by CNBC, 31 Jan 2021): https://www.cnbc.com/2021/01/31/melvin-capital-lost-more-than-50percent-after-betting-against-gamestop-wsj.html ; NPR, 18 Feb 2021: "Melvin Capital lost more than 50% of its investments in January": https://www.npr.org/2021/02/18/968829355/gamestop-hearing-today-roaring-kitty-along-with-ceos-to-appear-before-congress . Script: "about half its money that month".
8. 28 Jan 2021: "Robinhood, along with several other brokerages, restricts trading in GameStop ... after the regulatory deposit requirements for settling the securities skyrocket" (Reuters timeline). SEC report p. 15: "When retail broker-dealers restricted buying in certain stocks in January 2021...". Robinhood's CEO told Congress the restrictions "were put in place to meet increased regulatory requirements" (NPR). The script gives this as what the apps say.
9. After: "By February 3, GME's price was below $100 per share, and fell as low as $40.59 by February 19" (SEC report p. 22). 28 Jan to 3 Feb = six days: "within a week".
10. Not used on screen: Keith Gill ("Roaring Kitty") began posting YouTube videos on GameStop on 27 Jul 2020 (Reuters) and told Congress on 18 Feb 2021 "In short, I like the stock" and "I did not solicit anyone to buy or sell the stock for my own profit" (his written testimony: https://democrats-financialservices.house.gov/UploadedFiles/HHRG-117-BA00-Wstate-GillK-20210218.pdf).
