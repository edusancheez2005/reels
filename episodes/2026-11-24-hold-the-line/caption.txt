In January 2021, GameStop shares went from about $20 to $483 in 16 days. Then several trading apps switched off buying.
A price only counts while both sides can play.
On 12 January 2021 GameStop closed at $19.95. Funds had bet heavily that it would fall: more shares were sold short than the company had available to trade. Individual investors on an internet forum kept buying. It closed at $347.51 on 27 January and touched $483 the next day. Melvin Capital, one of the funds betting against it, took a $2.75 billion investment from two other funds on 25 January and was reported to have lost 53% that month. On 28 January, Robinhood and several other brokers restricted buying, saying the deposit they must post to settle trades had jumped. By 3 February the shares were under $100.
