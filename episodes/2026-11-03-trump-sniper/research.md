## Facts used (each with its source)

1. **Launch.** "On January 17, 2025, three days before his presidential inauguration, Donald Trump launched the $TRUMP memecoin."
   It ran on Solana, on the exchange Meteora, priced in USDC. Helius blog, "$TRUMP's Historic Weekend on Solana", 28 Jan 2025:
   https://www.helius.dev/blog/trump-solana-memecoin-records-trends-insights
2. **Announced with posts.** Trump announced it in posts on Truth Social and X on Friday 17 Jan ("My NEW Official Trump Meme is
   HERE!"). 200 million coins issued at launch, 800 million more over three years. CBS News, updated 20 Jan 2025:
   https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-launches-own-meme-coin-cryptocurrency
3. **The pool was public 17 hours early.** "Seventeen hours before the launch, 10% of the total $TRUMP supply was deposited as a
   single-sided liquidity position ... on Meteora's DLMM." (Helius, as 1.) A pool = the pot of coins that buyers trade against;
   once it's on Solana's public chain, anyone watching the chain can see it.
4. **The sniper's buy.** Bubblemaps (an on-chain analytics firm), working with YouTuber Coffeezilla: a trader bought $1.09 million
   of TRUMP "within the first second of trading", from an address beginning 6QSc2, split across at least nine wallets, and made
   "more than $100 million" (headline: $109 million). They say he paid an **$84,000 priority fee** to get the fast purchase.
   The Block, 18 Feb 2025 (Daniel Kuhn):
   https://www.theblock.co/post/341664/crypto-analytics-platform-bubblemaps-claims-one-trader-turned-1-million-into-109-million-trading-trump-memecoin
   Same claims, trade date 18 Jan 2025 (UTC), "$109 million in profits": CryptoPotato, 19 Feb 2025:
   https://cryptopotato.com/how-one-crypto-trader-sniped-trump-at-launch-and-made-109-million-bubblemaps-investigates/
5. **Coins and price he got.** Lookonchain (on-chain tracker), reported by The Daily Hodl, 27 Mar 2025: the whale "spent 1.09
   million USDC to buy 5.97 million TRUMP and booked a $108 million profit." → average price 1,090,000 / 5,970,000 = **$0.18 per
   coin** (my arithmetic). "Booked" = sold, so the profit was cashed out, not just on paper.
   https://dailyhodl.com/2025/03/27/whale-who-netted-108000000-profit-on-trump-books-loss-on-the-memecoin-after-presidents-truth-social-post
   ($1.09M + $108M profit ≈ $109M out, consistent with fact 4.)
6. **Who he is: unproven.** Bubblemaps linked the wallets to a pseudonymous trader but said the research can't definitively prove
   he's behind the trading ("too many connections to ignore"). He denies any inside tip; he says his team spotted an official
   Meteora address interacting with a TRUMP-USDC pool a day before launch. (The Block, as 4.) → **The reel does not name him.** It
   says "one trader", and the name card reads "identity unproven".
7. **Peak.** "climbing from under $10 on the morning of January 18th to a peak of $74.59 the following day" (Helius, as 1).
8. **Who paid.** Chainalysis, via CNBC (MacKenzie Sigalos), 6 May 2025: about 2 million wallets bought; "58 wallets made more than
   $10 million apiece, totaling roughly $1.1 billion in gains"; "About 764,000 wallets ... have lost money". The token hit about a
   $15 billion value after launch and lost most of it within days.
   https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/58-crypto-wallets-have-made-millions-on-trumps-meme-coin-764000-have-lost-money-data-shows/6253512/
   (The NBC News copy of the same CNBC article blocks automated reading.)
9. **New buyers.** Chainalysis (cited by Helius): "47% of buyers created their wallets on the same day they acquired these
   tokens." (Helius, as 1.) Used on screen only if it fits.

paying a huge fee to go first; everyone who bought after was buying from them.
