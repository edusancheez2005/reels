One trader turned about a million dollars into a hundred and nine million, by buying a meme coin in its first second.

The coin was TRUMP. Donald Trump announced it on Truth Social and X, three days before his inauguration.

How do you buy that fast? Coins like TRUMP trade from a pool, a pot of coins people buy from. TRUMP's pool showed up on the blockchain, a public record, seventeen hours before launch.

Traders called snipers run bots that buy the instant trading opens. This trader paid an eighty-four thousand dollar fee to go first. He got six million coins at eighteen cents each, and later cashed out.

Bubblemaps, an analytics firm, linked the trades to a specific trader, but can't prove it's him. He denies any inside tip, and says his team spotted the pool early.

So who paid for his profit? Everyone who bought later. About seven hundred and sixty-four thousand wallets, or accounts, lost money.

The pool was public hours before Trump's posts. If a post is how you hear about a coin, you're not early. You're who the snipers sell to.
