About 1.1 million bitcoin, believed to be Satoshi's, have never moved. What would it mean if they did? A thought experiment.
The 1.1 million is an estimate: in 2013 researcher Sergio Lerner traced one early miner's pattern in Bitcoin's first blocks. In May 2020, 50 coins mined in February 2009 moved, a bot said they might be Satoshi's, and bitcoin fell more than 7% that day. They didn't look like Satoshi's. A move only proves someone has the key. A signed message proves control without moving a coin: someone holding 145 addresses Craig Wright had claimed in court signed "Craig Steven Wright is a liar and a fraud", and in 2024 a UK judge ruled he isn't Satoshi.
If a headline says Satoshi is selling, look for a signature first.
