In 2019 he reported a burglary at his home in Georgia, and a small transfer to an exchange account in his name linked him to the coins. In November 2021 agents found 50,676 bitcoin in a floor safe and on a tiny computer under blankets in a popcorn tin. He was sentenced to a year and a day in prison.
