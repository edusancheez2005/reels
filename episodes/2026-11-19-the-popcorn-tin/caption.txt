A thief hid stolen bitcoin in a popcorn tin in his bathroom cupboard. Then he called the police about a burglary.
In September 2012 James Zhong tricked Silk Road's withdrawal system into paying out about 50,000 bitcoin. In 2019 he reported a burglary at his home in Georgia, and a small transfer to an exchange account in his name linked him to the coins. In November 2021 agents found 50,676 bitcoin in a floor safe and on a tiny computer under blankets in a popcorn tin. He was sentenced to a year and a day in prison.
