Hackers took about $6.3 billion from Binance on Thursday, most of it in about 20 minutes: the biggest crypto theft ever.
That's about 4x the old record.
Three blockchain firms (TRM Labs, Chainalysis, Elliptic) point to North Korea's TraderTraitor team. No government has confirmed it. Binance says it may have started with one engineer's compromised laptop. About $2.8B is frozen, and Binance says it will cover all customer losses.
Bitcoin is down about 5% since the hack, and Binance's own coin, BNB, about 6%. Crypto on an exchange is only as safe as that exchange's security, and its promise to pay you back.
