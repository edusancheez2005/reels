# The signing screen — Bybit, 21 Feb 2025 (morning skit, crypto, plan row Mon 23 Nov)

Topic source: plan (the trend scout's pick, the ~$12M Chainflip hack, did not clear RUNBOOK §0b: crypto-press coverage only, and
3 breaking reels were already made in the last 7 days; see insights/trends/2026-11-23.md).

## Facts (every number and step used on screen or in the voice)

1. **Date and target.** On 21 Feb 2025 attackers drained a Bybit Ethereum multisig cold wallet. Bybit's own timeline:
   "February 21, 2025, 14:13 UTC ... Hackers exploited the UI of the Safe multisig cold wallet ... musking [sic] the specific
   transaction." "Only a single Bybit cold wallet was compromised."
   https://www.bybit.com/en/learn/this-week-in-bybit/bybit-security-incident-timeline
2. **Amount (the one number).** FBI IC3 PSA I-022625-PSA (26 Feb 2025), headline "North Korea Responsible for $1.5 Billion Bybit
   Hack"; text: "theft of approximately $1.5 billion USD". Bybit's itemised total: "$1.46 billion" (401,347 ETH ≈ $1.12B,
   90,375 stETH, 15,000 cmETH, 8,000 mETH). Voice says "one and a half billion dollars" (FBI's approximate figure).
   https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250226 · Bybit timeline (above)
3. **401,347 ETH.** Bybit timeline ("401,347 ETH"); NCC Group: "more than $1.4 billion assets, including 401,347 ETH, drained
   from its cold wallet." Voice: "Four hundred and one thousand ether".
   https://www.nccgroup.com/research/in-depth-technical-analysis-of-the-bybit-hack/
4. **Several signatures; at least three.** NCC Group: the wallet needed "multiple approvals (in Bybit's case, at least three
   signers)"; the transaction could be sent once "3 signatures" were collected. (No source says exactly who signed; the
   script's "you / signer two / signer three" are the fiction.)
5. **What the signers saw vs. what it did.** Ben Zhou (Bybit CEO) on X, quoted by The Block, 21 Feb 2025: "all the signers saw
   the musked UI which showed the correct address" and "the signing message was to change the smart contract logic of our ETH
   cold wallet." NCC: signers reviewed "what appeared to be a routine internal transfer" but were "actually approving a request
   that handed over control of the cold wallet smart contract to the attackers." → voice: "The screen shows a routine
   transfer. Underneath, the transaction hands them the vault." / "You check the address."
   https://www.theblock.co/post/342667/crypto-exchange-bybit-hacked-as-over-1-billion-worth-of-eth-leaves-wallets · NCC (above)
6. **The rigged website, two days earlier.** Bybit's forensic statement (26 Feb, quoted by SlowMist): "The malicious code was
   injected into Safe{Wallet}'s AWS S3 bucket on February 19, 2025." NCC: "injecting malicious JavaScript code into
   Safe{Wallet} UI through a compromised developer machine", changes "subtle and specifically targeted Bybit". Bybit timeline,
   26 Feb: "malicious JavaScript code on Safe{Wallet}'s platform". → "hackers rigged the signing website two days ago".
   https://slowmist.medium.com/bybits-1-5-billion-theft-unveiled-safe-wallet-front-end-code-tampered-84b78f0fa9c2
7. **They wiped their code afterwards.** Bybit's statement via SlowMist: the attackers "removed the malicious code from the AWS
   S3 bucket to cover their tracks" about two minutes after the transaction. → "Then the hackers wipe their code."
8. **Attribution (one line in the voice only; kept out of the hook, cover and caption).** FBI PSA: "North Korea ... was
   responsible for the theft"; activity called "TraderTraitor". → "The FBI blamed North Korea."
9. **Customers covered.** Ben Zhou, 21 Feb: "Bybit is Solvent even if this hack loss is not recovered ... we can cover the
   loss." Bybit timeline, 24 Feb 09:12 UTC: Bybit "fully closed the ETH gap of client assets within 72 hours" (after "$1.23
   billion in ETH through bridge loans, whale deposits and OTC purchases"). → "Bybit refilled the ether within three days."
10. **Bybit is a big exchange.** The Block covers it as "Crypto exchange Bybit"; BitMEX Research estimated "around 75% of Bybit
    users' ETH deposits were drained" (The Block). "giant" is description, no figure on screen.
11. **The step.** NCC's recommendations: hardware wallets that display what is being signed (EIP-712) instead of blind-signing;
    Safe after the hack: "users should exercise extreme caution and remain vigilant when signing transactions." → "Before you
    sign anything, read the wallet's own screen, not the website."

Conflicts noted: Bybit's 21 Feb wording said "phishing"; later forensic reports (Sygnia/Verichains via Bybit, Safe, NCC) say a
compromised Safe developer machine and injected JavaScript. Safe's early statement said no codebase compromise; its later one
says a developer's machine was infiltrated. We say only "rigged the signing website", which every later source supports.
FBI says "TraderTraitor"; "Lazarus" is other firms' label, not used.

Receipt (Shot): NCC Group's "In-Depth Technical Analysis of the Bybit Hack" (10 Mar 2025), captured from the live page
(theblock.co and bybit.com refused the screenshot browser: a Cloudflare check and "upstream request failed"). Marks: the
"On 21st February 2025 ... 401,347 ETH ... cold wallet" line and the "routine internal transfer ... handed over control" line.
