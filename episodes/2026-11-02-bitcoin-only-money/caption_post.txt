If Bitcoin were the only money, the world would share one network handling about 7 payments a second.
Visa can handle 65,000. A thought experiment, not a prediction. We follow Maya through one day: her coffee payment waits in a queue (the average fee hit $128 on 20 April 2024), no bank can cancel a payment, bitcoin's price fell 64% in 2022, and a lost recovery phrase can't be reset. Supporters point to the fixed supply, and Lightning makes small payments near-instant.
Two of her problems are real today: payments are final, so double-check before you send, and keep two offline copies of your recovery phrase.
