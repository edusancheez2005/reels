In six days, Microsoft, America's air traffic control, Chase bank and Apple all broke, and officials had to say: this is not an attack.
Thursday: Outlook and Teams go down worldwide. Microsoft blames a network upgrade.
Friday: the FAA, which runs air traffic control, stops every departing flight in America. Only once before since nine eleven.
Monday: Chase payments slow down. Chase says it's not a cyberattack.
Tuesday: iCloud, the App Store and Apple Pay stall. Apple blames an internal update.
Same day, a classified network at the NSA, America's spy agency, goes dark during routine maintenance.
America's intelligence chief, Jay Clayton, says: "This is not a cyberattack."
His view: A.I. now finds security holes faster. Fixing them means taking systems down and back up, and he says that's when things like this happen.
If one app or card is your only way to pay, one bad update stops you, like Apple's did. Keep a second way to pay.
