   worldwide. Microsoft's final statement (13 Nov): "This was a technical issue not related to cyber or AI / agents. The incident
   was introduced by an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade..."
   https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/massive-microsoft-365-outage-disrupts-outlook-teams-copilot-worldwide/
3. **FAA, 13 Nov:** nationwide ground stop, "only the second in the U.S. since the Sept. 11 attacks"; cause "communication issues
   impacting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers"; Homeland Security Secretary Mullin: no indication it was a threat.
   https://www.wrdw.com/2026/11/14/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-second-time-since-911-what-know/?outputType=amp

   https://chicago.suntimes.com/business/2026/11/16/a-technical-issue-at-jpmorgan-chase-triggers-online-banking-delays-across-major-us-banks

5. **Apple, Tuesday 17 Nov:** "Affected services include the App Store, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple Support, and more" (9to5Mac,

   https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/11/17/74448/apple-confirms-what-caused-widespread-outage-affecting-millions-of-users/

6. **NSA, Tuesday 17 Nov:** an NSA internal classified communications system "temporarily went down on Tuesday"; NSA said it

## Lesson (the story's own)
Every company named its own change: an upgrade, an update, maintenance. Nobody broke in; the fixes broke things. For the viewer:
when everything lives on one app or one card, one bad update locks you out, so keep a second way to pay. (Advice, no number, no
claim to source.)
