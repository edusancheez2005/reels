## Facts used (every on-screen and spoken claim)

1. **6 May 2010: almost a trillion dollars, in minutes.** REUTERS-2020: the Dow "briefly plunged over a 1,000 points, temporarily
   wiping out nearly $1 trillion in market value." BLOOMBERG-2020: the flash crash "erased $1 trillion in market value in minutes,
   authorities said." HEARING (Chairman Kanjorski): "erasing more than $1 trillion in capitalization before recovering."
   REPORT: major indices "suddenly plummeted a further 5-6% in a matter of minutes before rebounding almost as quickly."
   DOJ-2015: "the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell by approximately 600 points in a five-minute span".
   → Script: "In May 2010, almost a trillion dollars vanishes from the US stock market in minutes". Tags: "6 May 2010", "≈ $1 trillion".
   (The trillion is market value that disappeared temporarily, per two major outlets; the regulators' report itself gives percentages.)
2. **About twenty minutes later most of it was back.** REPORT: the broad markets fell "Between 2:41 p.m. and 2:45:28 p.m."; "By
   approximately 3:00 p.m., most securities had reverted back to trading at prices reflecting true consensus values."; indices
   "'recovered' to close at losses of about 3% from the prior day." 2:41/2:45 → 3:00 is 15–19 minutes (my arithmetic).
   → Script: "Within twenty minutes, most of it is back."
3. **What he did: spoofing.** DOJ-2015: spoofing is "a practice of bidding or offering with the intent to cancel the bid or offer
   before execution."; he placed "multiple, simultaneous, large-volume sell orders at different price points"; "modified these
   orders frequently"; "typically canceled the orders without executing them." DOJ-2016: he "placed thousands of orders that he
   did not intend to trade, or 'spoof orders,'" "to create the appearance of substantial false supply and demand." CFTC-2015:
   "four to six exceptionally large sell orders"; "close to $200 million worth of persistent downward pressure".
   → Script: "Someone keeps placing enormous orders to sell, then changing them before anyone can buy. Fake orders, to push the price down. That's spoofing." (sell orders = "downward pressure", CFTC-2015)
4. **81,000 changes on crash day.** CFTC-2016 (the consent order's findings): "The program placed orders that were modified over
   81,000 times that day, with only 81 lots resulting in executed trades."
   → Script: "On crash day: eighty-one thousand changes." Tag: "81,000 changes".
5. **Five years.** 6 May 2010 → arrest on 21 April 2015 (fact 7). → "Five years, you hunt a mastermind." ("you", the agent and
   the hunt are the fiction; the reel does not say how the real investigators found him.)
6. **A bedroom in his parents' house, in a London suburb.** BLOOMBERG-2020: "using a computer in the bedroom of his parents house,
   where he'd lived since childhood." DOJ-2015: "Navinder Singh Sarao, 36, of Hounslow, United Kingdom". CFTC-2015: "Scotland Yard
   took Sarao into custody today, at his residence in London." Hounslow is an outer borough of west London.
   → Script: "a bedroom. In his parents' house." Tag: "Hounslow, London". "[suit] We brought helicopters." is the cartoon agent's line (fiction, pays off the volcano lair); no real raid is described.
7. **The real person, the arrest, the plea.** DOJ-2015: arrested "today in the United Kingdom" (21 April 2015). DOJ-2016: he
   "pleaded guilty to one count of wire fraud and one count of spoofing" (9 Nov 2016); "at least $12.8 million in illicit gains."
   → Script: "It's a real case: a trader, Navinder Sarao, arrested April 2015. He pleaded guilty."
8. **Contributed, not caused alone.** DOJ-2015 headline: "Contributing to the May 2010 Market 'Flash Crash'"; CFTC-2015: his
   conduct "contributed to market conditions that led to the Flash Crash"; CFTC-2016: "contributed to an extreme order book
   imbalance". REPORT (2010) names a different trigger: "a large fundamental trader (a mutual fund complex) initiated a sell
   program to sell a total of 75,000 E-Mini contracts (valued at approximately $4.1 billion)".
   → Script: "Regulators said he contributed to the crash, not caused it alone." (the plan's wording rule)
9. **The sentence.** REUTERS-2020: "time already served in jail of four months, with a year of home confinement". DECHERT: "time
   served" with "one year of home confinement" (28 Jan 2020, Judge Virginia Kendall, Chicago). BLOOMBERG-2020: "must serve a
   year of home detention in London".
   BLOOMBERG-2020 headline: "gets no jail for spoofing".
   → Script: "The sentence: a year confined to his home." (plus the four months he had already served in a London jail in 2015, which the caption states) Stonks' "Which room?" is a question, not a claim
   about where he served it.
10. Not used in the voice, for the caption: CFTC-2016 "$25,743,174.52 civil monetary penalty and $12,871,587.26 in disgorgement"
   (headline: "More than $38 Million"); REPORT: trades "at prices of a penny or less, or as high as $100,000".

Left out on purpose: his health diagnosis (reported by Bloomberg; not ours to joke near), what he did with the money, and any
