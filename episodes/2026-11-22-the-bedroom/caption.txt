In May 2010, nearly $1 trillion vanished from the US stock market in minutes. The trail led to a bedroom in a London suburb.
On 6 May 2010 the Dow briefly fell about 1,000 points, and most of it came back within about 20 minutes. In April 2015, Navinder Sarao of Hounslow was arrested for "spoofing": placing huge sell orders he never meant to fill. On crash day his program changed its orders more than 81,000 times. He pleaded guilty in 2016. US regulators said his trading contributed to the crash; their 2010 report pointed to one fund's $4.1 billion sale as the trigger. His sentence in 2020: the four months already served, plus a year of home confinement.
