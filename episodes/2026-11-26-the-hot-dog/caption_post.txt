Costco's hot dog and drink has cost $1.50 since 1985. US prices have roughly tripled since then.
When a price stands still, it's the money that moved.
If the combo had kept up with inflation it would cost about $4.70 today (US consumer prices, 1985 average to October 2026). Costco says it sold over 245 million of them in its 2025 financial year, and it makes its own hot dogs to hold the price. Its CEO said in March 2026 that the price will not change as long as he's around.
The suit guy and his friend are made up. The numbers are real.
