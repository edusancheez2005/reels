$2,861 a coin, then less than a tenth of a cent five minutes later, and buyers of the Squid Game token couldn't sell.
In October 2021 a team nobody could trace launched SQUID. To sell it you needed Marbles, a second coin that only came from a game the team promised and never released. Three days before the crash, CoinMarketCap warned that buyers couldn't sell. People kept buying. On 1 Nov 2021 the team cashed out an estimated $3.38M and the website vanished. John Lee, 30, from Manila, lost the $1,000 he put in.
The step: before you buy a new coin, check its trades for other people actually selling it. A price means nothing if you can't sell at it.
