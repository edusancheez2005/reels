This cod was caught off Scotland. Before it reached a British supermarket, it went to China. And back. Here's why. The catch is frozen, packed into a shipping container, and sent through the Suez Canal. In China, it's thawed, and filleted by hand. Every pin bone, pulled out one by one. Then it's frozen again, and shipped all the way home. Weeks at sea. Twice. For one fillet. Why? Labour. When reporters exposed this in two thousand and nine, Chinese factory workers were paid under a pound a day. So shipping it across the world was still cheaper than filleting it in Britain. By twenty twenty-two, China had overtaken Norway as Britain's biggest seafood supplier. So your cod and chips may have seen more of the world than you have.
