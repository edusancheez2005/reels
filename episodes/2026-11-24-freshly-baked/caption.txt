Last night Circle minted 1 billion new USDC on Solana in under seven minutes: four batches of 250 million each.
Before you hold a stablecoin, find out who makes it and read its monthly reserve report.
Between 19:13 and 19:20 UTC on 23 Nov, the same treasury account went from about 1 billion to 2 billion USDC. How it works: a Circle customer pays in dollars, Circle mints the same number of coins, and the dollars sit in cash and short-term US government debt. When someone redeems, the coins are burned. A mint on its own isn't a price signal: in the week before, total USDC supply actually fell by about $607 million.
