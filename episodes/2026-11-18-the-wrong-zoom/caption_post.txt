In March 2020, the US regulator froze a stock called ZOOM for two weeks. It wasn't the Zoom everyone was using.
Zoom Video, the video-call company, trades as ZM. The ticker ZOOM belonged to Zoom Technologies, which last reported an office in Beijing and had published nothing since 2015. Its shares went from about $3 to over $20 in a month. Then the SEC suspended trading from 26 March to 8 April 2020, citing concerns about investors confusing the two. The same mix-up happened in April 2019.
