In 2013, a man in Wales threw away a hard drive with the key to 8,000 bitcoin (his figure). Today: over $600 million.
The coins sit on a public record, but only the key moves them. A UK court ruled the drive became the council's property once it was dumped.
Write down your recovery words: they're the spare copy of your key.
