In twenty thirteen, a man in Wales threw away a hard drive. The bitcoin on it is worth over six hundred million dollars today. James Howells says it held the key to eight thousand bitcoin, the secret code that moves them. It went to a landfill in Newport. He offered the council a quarter of it to dig. They said no. In twenty twenty-five, a judge ruled the drive became the council's once it was dumped. Without that key, nobody can move the coins. Write down your recovery words: the spare copy of your key.
