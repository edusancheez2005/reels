There's a plastic wall inside your phone thinner than most human hairs. If it fails, your phone can catch fire. 🔥

It keeps your battery's two sides apart, so electrons have to take the long way round, through your phone. Pierce it, and they take the shortcut. That's what happened inside some Galaxy Note 7 batteries in 2016.
