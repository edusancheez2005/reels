In 2013, James Howells threw away a hard drive with the only key to 8,000 bitcoin (his figure). Today: about $600 million.
He found two drives in a drawer and binned the wrong one. The council refused to dig, he sued, and in 2025 a judge ruled that anything dumped at the landfill belongs to the council.
His two mistakes: one copy of the key, and keeping that drive next to a blank one. Keep your wallet's recovery words in two safe places, and label the device that holds your key.
