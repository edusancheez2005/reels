## Facts (checked 27 Nov 2026, 04:00–04:40 London; every URL came from a web search)

1. **Launch date and makers.** Wikipedia: "It was introduced on December 6, 2013"; "created by IBM software engineer Billy Markus
   and Adobe software engineer Jackson Palmer"; Palmer in Adobe's marketing department in Sydney, Markus at IBM in Portland, Oregon.
   — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dogecoin
2. **Made as a joke.** Wikipedia: "launched the satirical cryptocurrency as a way to make fun of Bitcoin." Fortune/Bloomberg (11 Feb
   2021): Markus and Palmer "created the token as a joke". Al Jazeera/Bloomberg (5 May 2021): "started in 2013 as a joke based on the
   Shiba Inu breed of dog". — https://fortune.com/2021/02/11/dogecoin-price-rise-creator-market-cap/ ·
   https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2021/5/5/bb-dogecoins-value-is-soaring-is-it-time-to-take-it-seriously
   → "a joke coin", "with a dog on it".
3. **Palmer had the website first; Markus made it real.** Wikipedia: "Palmer had purchased the domain Dogecoin.com". Benzinga
   (29 Apr 2022, interview with Markus): "Markus gave himself a weekend project to create his own coin." He found dogecoin.com, where
   Palmer "made a joke coin but hadn't created a physical coin yet." — https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/21/07/22155866/exclusive-billy-markus-on-creating-dogecoin-the-negatives-of-cryptocurrency-nfts-and-elon-
   → "Your mate has a website for a fake coin with a dog on it." (the mate = Palmer's role; "you" = Markus's role)
4. **Two hours, copied code.** Benzinga: "The project took only a couple of hours to make as Doge was a fork from Bell's" (a Litecoin
   clone). Markus, quoted by The Independent (via inkl): "i made doge in like 2 hours". —
   https://www.inkl.com/news/i-made-doge-in-like-two-hours-dogecoin-creator-says-he-didn-t-consider-environmental-impact
   → "in two hours", "You copy another coin's code and swap in the dog."
5. **Month one: a million visitors.** Wikipedia: "within the first 30 days, there were over 1 million visitors to Dogecoin.com."
6. **Month two: the Jamaican bobsled team.** NPR (22 Jan 2014): "The Dogecoin effort produced around $30,000 for the bobsled team",
   alongside other online campaigns (the team raised more than $184,000 in total). Wikipedia: "$36,000 worth of Dogecoin was donated"
   by the second day. — https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2014/01/22/265060754/wow-dogecoin-other-donors-send-jamaican-bobsled-team-to-sochi
   (NPR headline: "...send Jamaican bobsled team to Sochi": the 2014 Winter Olympics in Sochi → on-screen tag "SOCHI 2014".)
   → "Six weeks in, it helps send Jamaica's bobsled team to the Olympics" (helps: one of several sources). Launch 6 Dec 2013 → fundraiser 19–22 Jan 2014 ≈ six weeks.
7. **No limit: five billion new coins a year.** Wikipedia: Dogecoin "started with an intended supply limit of Ð100 billion"; "In
   February 2014, Palmer announced that the limit would not be added in the codebase"; infobox supply: "Unlimited (exactly Ð5 billion
   enter circulation each year)". → "You two scrap the limit. Five billion new coins a year, forever." (Feb 2014, the makers' decision.)
8. **2015: laid off, sold everything for about a used Honda Civic.** Markus, quoted by Cointelegraph (12 Feb 2021): "I gave away
   and/or sold all the crypto I had back in 2015 after being laid off" ... "for about enough in total to buy a used Honda Civic." Same
   quote in Fortune/Bloomberg (11 Feb 2021) and Business Insider. — https://cointelegraph.com/news/founder-of-8-4b-dogecoin-sold-everything-in-2015-for-a-used-honda-civic ·
   https://www.businessinsider.in/finance/news/dogecoins-creator-sold-all-his-coins-6-years-ago-after-getting-laid-off-and-says-he-is-stunned-by-the-mania-around-the-meme-based-token/articleshow/80880860.cms
   → "2015. Laid off, you sell all of yours for about a used Honda Civic." / "The Civic is Markus's own words."
9. **May 2021: over $85 billion, more than FedEx.** Wikipedia: "reaching a peak market capitalization of over US$85 billion on May 5,
   2021." Al Jazeera/Bloomberg (5 May 2021): "its value to $87 billion in Wednesday trading, according to CoinMarketCap.com data",
   "eclipsing the largest exchange-traded gold fund and even stocks like Fedex Corp. and Snap Inc." (URL in 2)
   → "over eighty-five billion dollars", "May 2021. The joke is worth more than FedEx." (past, dated; no price talk)
10. **Palmer left; critic's line.** Wikipedia: "Co-founder Jackson Palmer left the cryptocurrency community in 2015 and has no plans to
    return". Fortune: Markus, "Maybe it's that Dogecoin can be a good barometer for how far from reality things can get". (Background;
    the balanced-value line in the voice is "A coin's story isn't its value.")
11. **Markus no longer holds it (2021).** Business Insider: "I'm no longer part of the Dogecoin project, I left around 2015".
