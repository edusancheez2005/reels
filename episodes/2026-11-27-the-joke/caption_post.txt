Two engineers made Dogecoin as a joke in 2013. In May 2021 it was worth over $85 billion.
Before you buy any coin, ask who would buy it from you.
In December 2013, IBM engineer Billy Markus built the coin in a couple of hours from another coin's code, after Adobe's Jackson Palmer had put up a website for a joke coin with a dog on it. Within weeks it helped send Jamaica's bobsled team to the Sochi Olympics. Markus has said he sold everything in 2015, after being laid off, for about the price of a used Honda Civic.
The suit guy and his friend are made up. The numbers are real.
