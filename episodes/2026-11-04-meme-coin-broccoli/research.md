## Lesson (the why)
A meme coin has no business or cash flow, so the only reason its price holds is new buyers arriving. Early buyers (the 11
million-dollar wallets) profit by selling to later buyers; when buyers stop, the price falls (−93%) and most coins end with nobody
to sell to (1 in 70 kept $1,000 of liquidity; 62.5% of traders on that site had losses). "If a coin's only reason to exist is that
it's going up, you're the exit." No price prediction; the reel says nothing about where any coin goes next.
