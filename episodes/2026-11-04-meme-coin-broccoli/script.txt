In February 2025, CZ, who founded the crypto exchange Binance, posted his dog's name: Broccoli. A day later, a Broccoli coin made by strangers was worth one hundred and forty million dollars.
CZ said he wasn't making one.
It didn't matter. A meme coin needs only a joke or a famous name.
On a site called Four dot meme, anyone can make one with a form.
Within hours, dozens of Broccoli coins appeared. On the biggest one, eleven buyers made over a million dollars.
That coin is now down more than ninety percent from its peak.
Another site, Pump dot fun, saw over seven million coins. Only one in seventy kept a thousand dollars for sellers to cash out.
Late buyers lose. Early buyers sell to them, and when new buyers stop, the price falls.
Their mistake: buying a joke because it was rising. Ask: who buys this from me? If the answer is "it's going up", you'll be left holding it.
