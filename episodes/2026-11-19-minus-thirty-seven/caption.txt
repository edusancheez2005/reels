On 20 April 2020, the US oil price closed at minus $37.63 a barrel. Sellers were paying buyers to take oil away.
The price was for WTI crude due in May. Each contract is 1,000 barrels of real oil, collected in Cushing, Oklahoma, where the tanks were about 76% full and some of the empty space may already have been booked. With demand gone in lockdown and the contract ending the next day, holders who had nowhere to put oil paid to get out. It was the first time the price went below zero since trading began in 1983, and it touched minus $40.32 at about 2:30 p.m. New York time.
