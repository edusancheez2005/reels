# Pig butchering: the bank CEO who wired away $47.1M (research)

Plan row (Wed 11 Nov): pig-butchering, protection only, hook = the FBI IC3 yearly crypto-investment-fraud total.
Angle chosen: the real, fully documented case of Shan Hanes (Heartland Tri-State Bank), told as one person's story,
framed by the FBI's 2025 figure. Researched 11 Nov 2026, 04:00–04:40 UTC.

## Facts (each with its source)

1. **$7.2 billion**: "Cryptocurrency investment fraud was the highest source of financial losses to Americans in 2025 with
   $7.2 billion reported in losses." FBI IC3 2025 Annual Report, p. 11 (checked in the PDF from ic3.gov, 11 Nov 2026).
   https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
   On screen: "$7.2 BILLION", "lost to crypto investment scams, 2025", "FBI IC3 report". Screenshot: assets/ic3_report.png
   (page 11 crop, the "Cryptocurrency Investment Fraud" section).
2. **How these scams run** (pp. 11–12; the "taxes and fees" sentence is at the top of p. 12; Quote card in the reel): contact "through text messages, social media sites, advertisements, or dating
   applications", then a messaging platform; victims "are shown fake profits"; when they try to withdraw "they will be
   charged taxes and fees as a final attempt to exploit money from the victims before the scammers disappear". Run largely
   by organised crime in Southeast Asia using trafficking victims as forced labour. Same URL.
3. **"Pig butchering" defined** in a court filing in the Hanes case: "a scammer convincing a victim (a pig) to invest in
   supposedly legitimate virtual currency investment opportunities and then steals the victim's money — butchering the pig."
   CNBC (Dan Mangan), via NBC Washington, 21 Aug 2024:
   https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/business/money-report/cryptocurrency-pig-butchering-scam-wrecks-kansas-bank-sends-ex-ceo-to-prison-for-24-years/3699566/
   Script: "It's called pig butchering: fatten you up with fake profits, then take everything" (fake profits = IC3 fact 2; the name is from the court filing).
4. **Who Shan Hanes is**: CEO of Heartland Tri-State Bank (HTSB) in Elkhart, Kansas (town of ~2,000); had served on the
   board of the American Bankers Association and been chairman of the Kansas Bankers Association. DOJ press release
   19 Aug 2024 (screenshot assets/doj_head.png, doj_wires.png):
   https://www.justice.gov/usao-ks/pr/former-ceo-failed-bank-sentenced-prison ; chairman role: CNBC/NBC article above.
5. **How it started**: Hanes "began stealing after being targeted in a pig-butchering scheme in late 2022"; in December 2022
   his crypto buying "appeared to be precipitated by communication with an unidentified co-conspirator on the electronic
   messaging app 'WhatsApp'"; "the true identity of the co-conspirator, or conspirators, remain unknown" (prosecutors'
   filing, quoted by CNBC/NBC above). Script: "a stranger on WhatsApp".
6. **The trap**: he moved money "purportedly to buy cryptocurrency as the scammers insisted they needed more funds to unlock
   the supposed returns on his investments" (CNBC/NBC). Prosecutors: "Further 'investments' may be made, but always require
   another need for funds, to guarantee or unfreeze the earlier transfers." (court filing quoted by CNBC/NBC). Quote card.
7. **His own money first, incl. daughter's college fund**: "He also used $60,000 taken from a daughter's college fund"
   (defence filing, CNBC/NBC); also $40,000 from Elkhart Church of Christ and $10,000 from an investment club.
8. **11 wire transfers, $47.1 million, May–July 2023**: "Hanes initiated 11 outgoing wire transfers between May 2023 and
   July 2023 totaling $47.1 million of Heartland's funds to a cryptocurrency wallet in a cryptocurrency scheme referred to
   as 'pig butchering.'" DOJ release above. "over just eight weeks" (CNBC/NBC). Script: "in two months".
   Wire sizes (CNBC/NBC from court filings): first $5,000 (May 2023); $1.5M on 30 May and $1.5M the next day; two wires
   totalling $6.7M three days later; $10M less than two weeks later; $3.3M days afterwards. (Shown only as "11 wires".)
9. **Never saw a profit**: "Hanes never realized any profit, and lost all of the money he stole" (CNBC/NBC).
10. **The bank collapsed**: closed Friday 28 July 2023 by the Kansas Office of the State Bank Commissioner, FDIC named
    receiver: https://www.fdic.gov/resources/resolutions/bank-failures/failed-bank-list/heartlandtristate.html
    DOJ: "caused HTSB to fail and the bank investors to lose $9 million"; "The FDIC absorbed the $47.1 million loss."
    One of five US banks that failed in 2023 (CNBC/NBC).
11. **24 years**: sentenced to 293 months (24 years 5 months) on 19 Aug 2024 after pleading guilty to one count of
    embezzlement by a bank officer. DOJ release above. NBC headline: "sends ex-CEO to prison for 24 years" (assets/nbc_head.png).
12. FBI Operation Level Up (IC3 2025 report, p. 20): of 3,780 victims notified in 2025, "78% of those victims were unaware they
    were being scammed". Not used on screen (kept for the caption idea only if room).

## The steps (protection only, no how-to)
- A stranger who brings up crypto is the warning sign (IC3: contact starts by text/social/dating apps, then moves to a messaging app).
- A fee or "tax" before you can withdraw = the money is gone (IC3: "charged taxes and fees ... before the scammers disappear").
