A crypto scam took $47.1 million out of a Kansas bank through its own CEO. The bank failed, and he got 24 years.
Shan Hanes ran Heartland Tri-State Bank. In late 2022 a stranger on WhatsApp pulled him into a crypto "investment". Every payment needed another one to "unlock" his profits. He wired the bank's money out in 11 transfers over about 8 weeks and never got a cent back. The FBI says these scams cost Americans $7.2 billion in 2025.
Today's step: if a stranger brings up crypto, block them. If an app asks for a fee before you can withdraw, the money is already gone.
