# How Bitcoin started — sources & verification (checked 27 Oct 2026)

Hook: "Will Bitcoin go up or down? Nobody knows." Deliberately no price prediction (Sonar channel rule).

## Origin
- White paper "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System", Satoshi Nakamoto, 9 pages, posted 31 Oct 2008 to the
  metzdowd.com cryptography mailing list. [Certain] — https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf (page 1 rendered with pdf.js on
  bitcoin.org → assets/whitepaper-p1.png); date: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto
- "Banks are failing" (Oct 2008): global financial crisis; Lehman Brothers collapsed 15 Sep 2008. [Certain] (context, no figure on screen)
- Genesis block (block 0): 2009-01-03 18:15:05 UTC; coinbase text "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second
  bailout for banks". [Certain] — https://mempool.space/block/000000000019d6689c085ae165831e934ff763ae46a2a6c172b3f1b60a8ce26f
  and https://mempool.space/api/block/000000000019d6689c085ae165831e934ff763ae46a2a6c172b3f1b60a8ce26f
- First person-to-person transfer: 10 BTC from Satoshi to Hal Finney, block 170, 12 Jan 2009,
  tx f4184fc596403b9d638783cf57adfe4c75c605f6356fbc91338530e9831e9e16. [Certain] —
  https://mempool.space/tx/f4184fc596403b9d638783cf57adfe4c75c605f6356fbc91338530e9831e9e16
  Finney as programmer / "the first transaction from Nakamoto to him": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto
- Pizza: Laszlo Hanyecz, "I just want to report that I successfully traded 10,000 bitcoins for pizza." bitcointalk,
  22 May 2010 (two pizzas). [Certain] — https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=137.0 (screenshot assets/pizza-post.png,
  "Merited by" line and avatar hidden for legibility; text unchanged).
  Note: the script's "a year later" is loose (≈16 months after the Finney transfer).
- 10,000 BTC today: 10,000 × $79,770 ≈ $798M (price fetched earlier on 27 Oct 2026; at 12:55 UTC it was $79,517 → $795M,
  still "almost eight hundred million"). — https://mempool.space/api/v1/prices
- 2011: Nakamoto wrote to co-developer Mike Hearn that he had "moved on to other things" and was not heard from again.
  [Certain] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto (email published by Hearn; month commonly given as April 2011
  [Likely]; on-screen card says "April 2011").
- Estimated Satoshi holdings: 750,000–1,100,000 BTC, untouched since 2010 (estimate, Patoshi-pattern analysis). [Likely] —
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Nakamoto . On screen: "≈ 1,000,000 BTC · believed to be Satoshi's · never moved (estimate)".

## What gives it value
- Max supply 21,000,000 BTC (protocol issuance schedule; last fractions issued around 2140). [Certain]
- Issued supply: computed from the block subsidy schedule at tip height 973,285 (mempool.space/api/blocks/tip/height,
  27 Oct 2026) = 20,104,019 BTC; on-screen 20,103,946 is the same figure ~4 hours earlier. Left to issue ≈ 896,000. [Certain]
- "No one can print more" — issuance fixed by consensus rules. [Certain]
- "or freeze yours" — true only for self-custodied coins; exchange/custodial balances can be frozen. Qualified on screen:
  "*if you hold your own keys". [Certain with qualifier]
- "Millions of people agree it's worth something, like gold" — framed as the shared-belief argument; gold card says
  "Mostly valued by agreement" (gold also has jewellery/industrial demand). No user-count figure shown. [Likely]
- Critics: ECB economists Ulrich Bindseil & Jürgen Schaaf, ECB blog "ETF approval for bitcoin – the naked emperor's new
  clothes", 22 Feb 2024, argue bitcoin's fair value is still zero. Shown as a labelled paraphrase, not a quote. [Certain] —
  https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-02-22/bitcoin-fair-value-is-still-zero-despite-etfs-ecb-blogpost-says ;
  https://www.theblock.co/post/278546/bitcoin-failure-fair-value-zero-ecb-advisors
