313 times last year, the internet was deliberately shut down somewhere. What happens to bitcoin when it goes dark? A thought experiment, not a prediction.
Your coins sit on the blockchain, not in your phone, so a blackout doesn't erase them. Since 2017, Blockstream has beamed new Bitcoin blocks down from satellites, so a dish can still receive them. Sending is the hard part: a payment only counts once miners get it. In 2019, Coinkite co-founder Rodolfo Novak sent bitcoin by ham radio from Toronto to developer Elaine Ou in San Francisco, and Ou herself called it unrealistic because they set it up online first.
The step: your recovery words rebuild your wallet in any compatible wallet, even if the company behind yours is gone. Keep them on paper, in two places.
