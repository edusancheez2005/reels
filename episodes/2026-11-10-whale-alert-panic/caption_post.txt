$4.26 billion of bitcoin moved in one day and the internet said Strategy was selling. It wasn't. How to read a whale alert:
In Nov 2025 Arkham showed 43,415 BTC leaving Strategy's wallets. Arkham only knew about 484,000 of its coins; Strategy held over 640,000. The coins went to 100+ new wallets, and Arkham called it a custodian rotation. Michael Saylor said there was "no truth" to the rumour, and 3 days later an SEC filing showed Strategy had bought 8,178 more.
Before you panic: look up the transfer on a free explorer like mempool.space. Into an exchange? Someone may sell. Into new wallets? Often storage.
