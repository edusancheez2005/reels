In January 2009 Zimbabwe printed a 100 trillion dollar banknote. It barely covered a bus fare.
Don't count the zeros. Count what they buy.
The note has fourteen zeros and was worth about 30 US dollars on the street the day it came out. The central bank had kept printing money, and at the worst point, in November 2008, one economist estimates prices were doubling about every day. Zimbabwe stopped using its own dollar in April 2009. In 2015 the central bank swapped the old notes at 40 US cents each, while collectors on eBay were paying up to 35 dollars for one.
