Bitcoin miners get their pay cut in half about every four years, and nobody signs it off. It's called the halving.
In 2009 every new block paid 50 new bitcoin. Then 25 in 2012, 12.5 in 2016, 6.25 in 2020 and 3.125 since block 840,000 in April 2024. The code halves the new coins every 210,000 blocks, which is why only 21 million will ever exist. The next cut is due around 2028.
The suit guy and his "boss" are made up. The numbers are real.
