# The midterm dip and bounce: why crypto fell when the Democrats won, then ether jumped

Requested live by Edu (4 Nov 2026, after the midday scout): "Democrats did win, therefore Ethereum kind of went up... explain why".
He chose the angle "dip, then bounce" and to name the Democrats openly (AskUserQuestion, 4 Nov). Researched 4 Nov 2026, 21:30–22:00 UTC.
topic_source: breaking. Morning reel (meme-coin-broccoli) was plan, and 2 breaking reels went up in the last 7 days (30 Oct), so this is allowed.

Framing check: **no outlet says the Democratic win made ether rise.** Reuters and CoinDesk tie the win to the overnight DIP. The
rebound is put down to relief/"priced in", with stocks rebounding at the same time. The script says exactly that and nothing more.

     "Crypto tracks stocks higher after Democrat midterm wins", assets/reuters_head.png)

     Associated Press reported" (assets/unchained_body.png)
     https://unchainedcrypto.com/democrats-sweep-congress-as-sherrod-brown-survives-cryptos-ohio-spending-push/
2. **The dip.** CoinDesk (10:38 UTC): bitcoin was "recovering from a dip to around $75,600 near 03:00 UTC on Wednesday." "The initial
   dip in bitcoin came as U.S. midterm results showed Democrats winning control of Congress, coinciding with fresh questions over
   crypto legislation." Reuters and Decrypt: bitcoin touched a six-week low.

3. **Why it fell: the rules.** Reuters: "a potential blow to an industry that has sought faster deregulation for digital assets."
   Unchained: Elizabeth Warren, "who has long pushed for tighter crypto oversight, 'appears poised' to chair Senate Banking, Politico

   headline: "...But Crypto Faces Tougher Fight in Washington" (assets/decrypt_head.png).
   Script: "may now lead the Senate committee that oversees banks", "a big crypto law now looks even less likely".
4. **The Ohio race.** Unchained: "In Ohio, Democrat Sherrod Brown beat Republican Sen. Jon Husted despite heavy crypto spending

   Decrypt: "Ethereum, meanwhile, spiked 7% to $2,434". CoinGecko hourly: ETH $2,434 at 17:00 UTC (high of the day), $2,294 on
   3 Nov 18:00 UTC (+6.1%), +8.4% from the 03:00 low. BTC $80,491 at 17:00 UTC.
   - https://theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-04-bitcoin-price-midterm-elections-binance-hack-recovery-433030 (page blocks screenshots)

   Screen/voice: "nearly 6%" and "$2,434" (CoinGecko 17:00 UTC; The Block $2,433, Decrypt $2,434). Hook number: "+6%".
7. **DeFi led.** CoinDesk: "CoinDesk's DeFi Select Index (DFX) was the strongest performer, rising by around 5%". DeFi = apps that
   lend and trade crypto without a bank. Script: "Ether, and the coins of apps built on it, bounced hardest" — CoinDesk's DeFi
   index led (DeFi apps mostly run on Ethereum; we say "apps that lend and trade without a bank" and don't claim more).
8. **Why it bounced: priced in.** Decrypt: "Analysts who spoke to Decrypt said the Democratic sweep had largely been priced into
   crypto markets before election night." Nexo (via The Block): "Markets took the Democratic win in stride... Futures fell as much

   the growth story." Quote card (Decrypt words, verbatim fragment): "largely been priced into crypto markets before election night".
9. **Not just crypto.** Reuters headline: "Crypto tracks stocks higher after Democrat midterm wins". Schwab (9:12 a.m. ET):

## Deliberately not used
- "Democrats winning made ETH go up": no source says it. The reel says the win caused the dip and the bounce was relief + stocks.
- Exact AP call times, seat counts, flipped states (sources disagree).
- The Binance hack as a cause (Reuters mentions it; covered on 30 Oct; Hougan says its effect was in line with risk assets). Left out to keep one thread.
- Anything about where prices go next. The lesson is about how markets price expected news, not a trade.
- Reuters' $68M (pre-vote) — used the lower post-vote Politico $55M.

## Format candidates
- **documentary** (picked): a news-desk story told through real headlines, a real price chart and the numbers between them. The
  story IS the headlines and the chart. Differs from the morning reel (motion) and the last 3 posted (motion, object, character).
- motion: the dip-and-bounce as a kinetic chart only — loses the receipts that make it credible.
- scenario: a trader up at 3 a.m. watching results — fun, but invents a person for a news story.
