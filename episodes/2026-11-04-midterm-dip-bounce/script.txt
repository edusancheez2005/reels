Ether, Ethereum's coin, jumped nearly six percent on Wednesday. But hours earlier it fell, as results showed the Democrats winning the US Congress.
On election night, bitcoin slid to about seventy-five thousand six hundred dollars.
Why the drop? Crypto companies want looser rules, and this result means a tougher fight. In Ohio, they spent over fifty-five million dollars trying to beat Democrat Sherrod Brown in a Senate race. He won anyway.
And Elizabeth Warren, a senator who wants tighter crypto rules, may now lead the Senate's banking committee.
So why the bounce? Analysts told the news site Decrypt that traders mostly expected this result. The early counts caused a scare. Once the result was official, the guessing was over. Stocks and gold rose too.
The trap is reading one headline as the whole reason. Decrypt said it's hard to pin the bounce on politics alone. So before you believe "this news moved crypto", check what else moved that day.
