850,000 bitcoin, gone. In 2014 the world's biggest bitcoin exchange said it lost them. Repayments still aren't done.
Mt. Gox was a Tokyo website where people kept their bitcoin. US prosecutors say hackers got into its server in 2011 and took about 647,000 coins over two years, while customers only saw a balance on a screen. In Feb 2014 it froze withdrawals and filed for bankruptcy. In 2019 its boss, Mark Karpelès, was cleared of embezzlement but convicted of tampering with records. A court-appointed trustee started repaying in 2024; about 34,000 BTC still sit in Mt. Gox wallets, and the deadline is now Oct 2027.
The lesson: a balance on an exchange is a promise. Send a small test amount to a wallet only you control, so you know you can get out.
